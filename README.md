## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains a complete **Descriptive Analytics** study on a synthetic but realistic 30-day sales dataset of **Haldiram’s** – one of India’s most iconic packaged food & snack brands.

The analysis covers:
- Best-selling products (units & revenue)
- Daily sales trends & weekly seasonality
- Product ratings distribution
- Regional demand patterns
- Category-wise volume vs revenue performance
- Inventory health

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

1. What are the best-selling products by units and by revenue?
2. How are total sales trending day-over-day? Is there a weekly pattern?
3. How are products rated? Is rating quality slipping anywhere?
4. Which region generates the highest unit sales and revenue?
5. Which category leads in volume vs revenue?
6. Are the highest-volume and highest-revenue products the same?
7. Is there a growing or declining trend in sales volume?
8. Which SKUs are currently at risk of stocking out?
9. What is the single strongest day and strongest region–category combination?

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- **Peda**, **Soan Papdi** and **Moong Dal** dominate unit sales. Sweets (especially premium items + Gift Packs) drive the majority of revenue (₹8.60 Cr).
- Clear **weekend peaks** (Sat–Sun) and mid-week troughs (Tue–Wed), with a mild upward trend.
- Customer satisfaction is healthy and stable at **4.14 / 5**. No sustained decline observed.
- **North India** (164k units, ₹4.05 Cr) and **West India** lead demand.
- **Sweets** lead revenue; **Namkeen** leads pure volume.
- Inventory position is excellent – **0 SKUs** currently Low or Out of Stock.
